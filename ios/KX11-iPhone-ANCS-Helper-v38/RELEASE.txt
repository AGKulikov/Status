KX11 ANCS Helper v38
Bundle ID: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Build: 38
Marketing version: 38.0
Matched Android build: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1201

Architecture replacement:

- permanent Android link-anchor instead of rotating service UUIDs;
- one RequiresANCS Central owner with stable state restoration;
- stable-F04 PAIR/B3 current-link proof gates Android ANCS discovery on its direct client;
- no 15-second pending-connect/system-reconnect cancellation;
- restored `.connecting` is reconciled from same-owner F04 system-table or beacon proof, never
  from elapsed time alone;
- increasingly sparse read-only F04 table probes cover an ACL established after Helper startup;
- one evidence-driven cancel waits for didDisconnect/didFailToConnect, then reopens the same
  CBPeripheral with RequiresANCS and AutoReconnect;
- retrieveConnectedPeripherals is physical-link evidence only and cannot start protocol readiness;
- official restore -> known peripheral -> system-connected service -> scan resolution ladder;
- normal resolution claims a system-connected peer without disconnecting it; only the proven
  stale-restoration request uses the one-shot cancel/terminal-callback rescue;
- iOS 17+ system AutoReconnect has exclusive ownership while isReconnecting=true;
- manual reconnect waits for didDisconnect before issuing a new connect;
- Android owns ANCS discovery/CCCD after B3 and returns idempotent readiness via F05/B4;
- bundle identifier and non-versioned restoration identifiers are unchanged for in-place update.
