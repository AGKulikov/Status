KX11 ANCS Helper v26
====================

Companion release: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1187
Bundle identifier: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Marketing version: 26.0
Build number: 26
Minimum iOS version: 13.0

Release verification
--------------------

- Phase one connects to F04 with RequiresANCS=false and is the only phase that discovers B2/B3.
- The encrypted phase-one peer writes ANCS-HANDOFF before the intentional disconnect.
- Phase two reconnects to the same CBPeripheral with RequiresANCS=true.
- Phase two deliberately skips all custom F04 discovery and waits for Android's ANCS owner.
- The iPhone-owned B4 relay remains generation 5/F05 and proves the post-handoff owner.
- Core Bluetooth auto-reconnect remains disabled; the two transitions have one app owner.
- Restoration identifiers move to v26/g4/g5 so v25 callbacks cannot own the new state machine.
- Protected-data lock/unlock events still update B4 flag bit 5 immediately.
- The B4 frame remains A5/01, eight bytes, little-endian sequence and CRC-8/ATM.
- Classic Bluetooth profiles, names, pairing, the system LE bond and ANCS authorization remain
  untouched.
