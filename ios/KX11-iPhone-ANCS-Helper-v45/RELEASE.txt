KX11 ANCS Helper v45
Bundle ID: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Build: 45
Marketing version: 45.0
Matched Android build: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1211

Changes from v44:

- adds a cryptographically random, durable 128-bit Q keyed by exact owner UUID + protocol-2 F04
  publication nonce; persistence is synchronously reread before any Pair write;
- replaces managed Central plain Pair with exact 17-byte `[0x50][Q]` and requires exact current
  `[0x4C][Q]` alias binding before `[0x41][Q]` may confirm ANCS CCCDs;
- keeps legacy ASCII PAIR/ANCS only in the separate Peripheral diagnostic/bootstrap route;
- reuses the same Q for restored same-owner/same-nonce tuples and fails closed for malformed,
  unknown or unpersisted tuples without writing Pair;
- supports both Core Bluetooth restoration callback orders and a missing-restored-F05 case by
  binding the one-shot capability to the first exact current F05 generation;
- rebinds an existing P/Q+F04 transcript across F05 replacement without replaying P/B3/READY;
- clears unsubscribe/failed alias proof without adding Pair, cancel, connect, retry or recovery
  budget loops;
- preserves the v44 bundle/restoration IDs, F04/F05 UUIDs, adoption records and all v44 → v41
  recovery contracts.
