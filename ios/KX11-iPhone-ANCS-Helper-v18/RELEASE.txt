KX11 ANCS Helper v18
====================

Companion release: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1175
Bundle identifier: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Marketing version: 18.0
Build number: 18
Minimum iOS version: 13.0

Release verification
--------------------

- Default role is Peripheral and keeps the v11 iPhone_ANCS route.
- Central role scans only for Geely_ANCS and connects with RequiresANCS.
- Core Bluetooth system auto-reconnect is deliberately disabled. Every disconnect resolves a
  fresh filtered D2D9 advertisement before reconnecting, so stale B2/B3/B4 handles cannot race
  Android's same-peer ANCS handoff.
- PAIR plus the per-connection B3 challenge fixes the peer before KX11 performs same-peer ANCS
  attach; the Android 9 Classic-BONDED/LE-key-size deadlock is not used as the proof anymore.
- The first B3 authentication challenge is retried on the same physical connection; persistent
  errors are bounded and cause a fresh-advertisement reconnect.
- B4 telemetry is READ/NOTIFY in Peripheral mode. In Central mode KX11 sends a five-second B4
  notification that wakes the suspended Helper; Helper answers with a fresh write-with-response.
- Every BLE wake creates a fresh CoreTelephony snapshot, avoiding a retained foreground cache.
- Role changes stop the previous Core Bluetooth route before starting the next one.
- Background modes contain bluetooth-central and bluetooth-peripheral.
- The modern disconnect callback remains visible to the iOS 13 deployment target, as required by
  current Xcode SDK protocol-conformance checks.
- Classic Bluetooth profiles, pairing and names are untouched.
- A missing/invalidated D2D9 service or stale ATT handle forces a real disconnect and waits for a
  fresh advertisement instead of rediscovering cached handles once per second.
- Central D2D9 uses generation-2 UUIDs (`...2F02`) so iOS cannot reuse the generation-1 GATT
  handles left by earlier B4 schema changes.
- A complete current CBService cache is accepted first; otherwise only missing B2/B3/B4 UUIDs are
  requested, with a cache fallback if Core Bluetooth reports uuidNotAllowed.
- A saved/restored peripheral stuck in `connecting` is abandoned after 15 seconds and resolved
  again from a fresh generation-2 service advertisement.
- Repeated Central failures back off through 1/2/5/10/20/30 seconds and log NSError domain/code.
