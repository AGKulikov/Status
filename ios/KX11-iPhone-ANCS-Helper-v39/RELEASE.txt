KX11 ANCS Helper v39
Bundle ID: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Build: 39
Marketing version: 39.0
Matched Android build: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1201

Changes from v38:

- exact-owner encrypted B3 always writes ANCS-READY once; transient ancsAuthorized=false no
  longer deadlocks Android ANCS discovery;
- Android F05/B4 ANCS-SUBSCRIBED after both ANCS CCCDs is authoritative access proof and can
  complete green readiness even if the Core Bluetooth snapshot is stale;
- didUpdateANCSAuthorizationFor false invalidates old access/CCCD proof and turns readiness orange
  without tearing down the link; green returns only after a new current-owner proof;
- non-auto-reconnecting didDisconnect/didFail materializes one deferred exact-owner intent;
- a matching late didConnect consumes that intent and is accepted instead of cancelled;
- `.connecting` alone retains the sole deferred intent read-only and cannot consume it, claim
  ownership or issue a duplicate connect;
- restoration ownership claims are bounded to two; claim 2 requires exact
  retrieveConnectedPeripherals(F04) physical proof after a fresh connect omitted didConnect;
- beacon visibility and elapsed time alone cannot trigger claim 2;
- restoration and claim-2 proof are bound to the persisted Geely identifier; arbitrary restored
  owners are never silently persisted;
- a new didConnect starts B4=false and waits for exact current F05 didSubscribe; the only restore
  exception is a one-shot, two-sided proof combining the exact persisted `.connected` Central
  owner with the exact restored F05 object listing that same owner in subscribedCentrals;
- neither half of that restoration proof can establish readiness alone, and green still requires
  current B3/ANCS-READY plus Android ANCS-SUBSCRIBED;
- Central delegate callbacks and delayed discovery/security/wake work require exact current
  CBPeripheral object identity;
- local F04/F05 publication uses exact pending/published service objects plus a monotonic
  generation; a late same-UUID didAdd after stop/start or role change is ignored without clearing
  or removing the current generation;
- CBPeripheralManager restoration installs its exact restored service as a separate publication
  lineage while preserving the two-sided exact restored-F05 B4 proof;
- every delayed connection remains poweredOn/F05-gated;
- bundle identifier, restoration identifiers, saved Geely identity and system pairing are kept.
