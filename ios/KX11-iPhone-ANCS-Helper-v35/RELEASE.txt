KX11 ANCS Helper v35
Bundle ID: ru.natro.kx11ancshelper
Build: 35
Marketing version: 35.0
Matched Android build: Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1198

This is the single-owner ANCS release:

- RequiresANCS=true is present on the first and only Central connection.
- PAIR and B3 execute on the same encrypted ATT owner.
- ANCS-READY waits for `ancsAuthorized` and resumes only from
  `didUpdateANCSAuthorizationFor` on that same owner.
- ANCS-HANDOFF and the deliberate second connection are removed.
- iOS 17+ uses Core Bluetooth auto-reconnect; older iOS uses retained-owner backoff.
- System AutoReconnect and manual reconnect are mutually exclusive; no competing connect call.
- A .connecting owner that has made no progress for 15 seconds is cancelled by the watchdog.
- The watchdog invalidates the stale namespace and waits for a fresh D2D9 advertisement before
  reconnecting, so no second Central owner is opened in parallel.
- stopScan/cancelPeripheralConnection are deferred until CBCentralManager is poweredOn; the
  API-MISUSE restoration race is removed.
- A generation rejected with uuidNotAllowed is quarantined until Android advertises a genuinely
  new namespace; reconnect backoff no longer resets on a merely connected but invalid ATT owner.
- Core Bluetooth managers are created synchronously on background restoration launch.
- Central and Peripheral state restoration use stable, non-versioned identifiers.
- The selected role, Android peripheral identifier and dynamic namespace persist across relaunch.
- Ordinary disconnect preserves the reverse F05/B4 GATT server and namespace.
- Android B4 wake notifications keep battery/network sampling alive while Helper is backgrounded.
- Green requires iOS ANCS authorization, Helper confirmation, both ANCS CCCDs, B4 CCCD,
  battery and network data.
