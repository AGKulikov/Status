# Status Widget HA1170

Date: 2026-08-05
Version name: `v2.8.2-ha1170`
Version code: `208021170`

## Notification text alignment

- Sender, time, application and message fields retain their independent top/centre option.
- The option now controls the actual amount of text received, not only the maximum-lines viewport.
  One line in a three-line field can therefore sit at its top edge or be centred; two lines behave
  the same way without changing the configured field or line limit.
- The choice is applied identically to ellipsis and automatic-scroll modes and remains persisted
  across editor reopen and style copy.

## Stable music artwork hand-off

- On a track boundary the fingerprint of the previous rendered cover is retained for the whole
  lifetime of the next track.
- A late MediaSession/cache packet can no longer produce the visible old-cover flash after the
  correct new cover has already appeared.
- Adjacent tracks which explicitly name the same album may still share one album cover.

## Included previous fixes

- Real notification-card and application-icon alpha rounding, independent icon proportions,
  notification borders and saved layout geometry remain included.
- Physical climate-shortcut centring remains based on the complete painted button rectangle.
- Helper v17 and HA1169 generation-2 GATT/B4 wake polling remain protocol-compatible and unchanged.
