  `com.ecarx.hud/.hud.HomeActivity` footprint: `808×266 @ (0,720)`.

- Verifies that both named layers appear in the live `dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --list` output and
  reports `маска ВКЛ/ВЫКЛ` plus the verification result in the HUD runtime status.

  composite display.
