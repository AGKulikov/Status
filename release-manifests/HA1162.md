# Status Widget HA1162

Package: `ru.natro.statuswidget`
Version name: `v2.8.2-ha1162`
Version code: `208021162`

## Selectable iPhone BLE role

- `Peripheral` remains the default and preserves the HA1161 route: iPhone Helper advertises
  `iPhone_ANCS`, while KX11 is the BLE central/GATT client.
- `Central` is opt-in: KX11 advertises the application-owned `Geely_ANCS` service and iPhone
  Helper initiates the BLE link with the public ANCS-required connection option.
- The two routes are mutually exclusive and the selected role must match in Status Widget and
  Helper 12. Classic HFP/A2DP/PBAP pairing is not renamed, disconnected or repurposed.

## Reverse-route identity and recovery

- KX11 accepts `PAIR`, requires an encrypted `SECURE` ATT operation and attaches its ANCS GATT
  client to that exact incoming iPhone peer on the same physical BLE link.
- The verified LE identity is stored separately from the selected Classic address and reused for
  recovery after a confirmed BLE loss.
- Ambiguous or Classic-only ACL events do not tear down the reverse BLE route.

## Helper 12

- Adds a persistent `Peripheral / Central` selector while leaving Helper 11 untouched.
- Central mode uses service-filtered discovery, Core Bluetooth state restoration,
  `CBConnectPeripheralOptionRequiresANCS` and system auto-reconnect on supported iOS versions.
- B4 telemetry is written over the encrypted application channel in Central mode and remains
  READ/NOTIFY in the existing Peripheral mode.
