## Android / head unit

- The configured media delay is anchored to the boot/QuickBoot boundary and also runs on a
  dedicated in-process exact timer. It no longer waits behind launcher/service startup phases;
  AlarmManager remains the process-death fallback.
- Media-session discovery retries at 150/250/400/700 ms and then up to two seconds instead of
  entering the old 0.5/1/2/4/8-second ladder.
- A dedicated Topic Specific APNs `.p8` can be imported after installation. The plaintext is
  encrypted by Android Keystore in no-backup storage and is absent from APK resources and Git.
- Natro sends two ActivityKit starts only on the selected iPhone's Classic Bluetooth rising edge
  and immediate end events on the falling edge.
- Helper tokens/configuration use a separate CRC-protected ATT20 magic over the authenticated C5
  owner and can never be interpreted as vehicle commands.
- Torn C5 synchronization recovers in roughly two seconds instead of the previous worst-case
  twenty-second HELLO/coalescing interaction.

## Helper 63

- Live Activity and Dynamic Island buttons retain their App Group/LiveActivityIntent command path.
- Vehicle controls update optimistically at tap time and roll back on explicit failure/timeout.
- Command-driven ActivityKit updates flush immediately; unsolicited state is coalesced at 500 ms.
- APNs push-to-start and per-activity tokens are observed and provisioned to Natro; local start/end
  remain bounded fallbacks and prevent orphaned cards.
