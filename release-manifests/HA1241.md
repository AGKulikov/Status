## Recovery changes

- The first enrolled attempt remains a fast `autoConnect=false` direct connect. After a proven
  terminal callback, the next attempt enters the existing enrolled recovery scan instead of
  retrying the saved locator blindly.
- The enrolled recovery scan is now genuinely unfiltered. It accepts only a uniquely selected
  Classic bond whose stack-resolved BLE facade equals the saved bonded enrollment identity; name,
  RSSI and untrusted advertising data never become identity proof.
- A completely silent `connectGatt` owner is never closed speculatively. After the bounded
  30/60/120-second same-wrapper ladder, Natro runs an unfiltered presence window and periodically
  reasserts that same wrapper. An exact resolved advertisement brings the reassert forward. This
  closes the permanent `WAIT_SYSTEM_CONNECTION` trap without allocating a second `clientIf`.
- Top-level recovery no longer stops or resets merely because Classic/HFP drops. Classic remains a
  liveness accelerator, not a prerequisite for ANCS recovery. Recovery counters reset only after
  Route A reaches READY.
- Retry, retained-owner and top-level backoff timers receive SecureRandom ±20 percent jitter to
  avoid deterministic resonance with ECARX and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth coexistence windows.
- On Android 9 only, repeated status 133 or INVALID_HANDLE evidence requests a guarded reflective
  GATT cache refresh immediately before a proven registered owner is closed. A first failure,
  silent/unprovable owner or newer Android version never triggers it.

## Diagnostics and boundaries

Route journal entries include epoch, acquisition mode, reducer phase, recovery class, exact owner
and operation cursors, failure count, emitted effects and delay. Scan mode/filter count, GATT cache
failure count and guarded refresh outcome are logged without addresses, keys or payloads.

No guessed CarPlay gate is included: this source tree has no trustworthy projection-session signal,
and treating ordinary Bluetooth loss as CarPlay would suppress valid autonomous recovery. Radio-off
already pauses Route A and requires a fresh epoch when the adapter returns.

The production topology remains Route A and retains the one-owner, epoch and encrypted C4/H proof
invariants. Package, preferences, system pairs, enrollment material, Helper wire, API 28 target and
stable update certificate are unchanged. Portable tests and CI do not replace the physical KX11 /
iPhone acceptance matrix: 20 reconnects, radio off/on, more than 20 minutes out of range, iPhone
reboot/unlock and one real notification after both ANCS CCCDs plus valid B4 telemetry.
