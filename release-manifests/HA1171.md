## Notification application-icon rounding

- The icon is rendered through one four-corner round-rect using the current physical bounds of
  the `ImageView`; the same path is used by the live overlay and the settings preview.
- The old rounded output bitmap cache was removed. It could be stretched after a grid resize and
  explicitly recycled while Android 9 still had its texture queued, producing a flat top,
  over-rounded bottom or a shape that changed after reopening settings.
- The radius is clamped to half of the current smaller side, so every permitted value remains a
  mathematically valid rounded rectangle.
- The notification layout editor no longer paints its generic square blue item fill over the
  icon's transparent corner pixels. Grid, exact bounds and resize handles remain visible.

## Included previous fixes

- Independent top/centre alignment for the actual amount of notification text remains included.
- Stale previous-track artwork remains blocked for the whole current track.
- Physical climate-button centring and the generation-2 helper/telemetry transport are unchanged.
