and Helper 53 remain unchanged.

## Confirmed failure

The 2026-08-21 KX11 journal proves that the same enrolled iPhone was healthy and delivered 402
ANCS source records before the route failed. A C5 write lost its Android callback, yet the adapter
cleared the local pending marker and later rejected a new ANCS Control Point request. Recovery then
allocated the exact saved LE identity with `autoConnect=true`; Android 9 retained that wrapper for
more than 56 seconds without any callback. After a fresh active attempt with `autoConnect=false`,
the same phone connected in 213 ms and resumed ANCS normally.

## Repair

- Every initial and recovery attempt to the exact saved, bonded LE identity now uses
  `connectGatt(autoConnect=false, TRANSPORT_LE)`.
- The single-owner fence remains intact: a registered predecessor is drained before one replacement
  owner is allocated; there is no scan, name, topology, pair deletion or second-wrapper fallback.
- An ANCS Control Point request arriving behind optional telemetry, battery or C5 work is retained
  behind the one ATT slot instead of being falsely reported as rejected.
- A missing C5 write callback no longer clears an operation whose terminal state is unknown. The
  registered owner is reset, recovery is active, and optional C5 resubscription receives a bounded
  5–30 minute backoff so it cannot repeatedly destabilize the primary notification channel.
- Successful optional-operation callbacks drain deferred ANCS before additional C5 or battery work.
