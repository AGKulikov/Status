Package: `ru.natro.statuswidget`
Version name: `v2.8.2-ha1160`
Version code: `208021160`

## Cold iPhone ANCS recovery

- A bonded `iPhone_ANCS` now registers one long-lived
  `connectGatt(autoConnect=true, TRANSPORT_LE)` owner after process start/package replacement.
- The cold owner is not destroyed by a short connection timeout; Android 9 may retain the
  background request until the bonded iPhone's rotating private address is resolved through the
  system bond.
- A 2.5-second stack-quiescence window prevents the replacement process from racing the GATT
  client registration owned by the APK process that Android just removed.
- Explicit failures retry the same serialized background path with bounded backoff. GPS-style
  scan/direct connect remains the fallback for an unbonded/bootstrap peer.
- Once a GATT owner has connected successfully, radio loss keeps and re-arms that exact owner;
  no second competing client is created.
