# Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1196 + KX11 ANCS Helper v33

Matched release for the KX11 Android 9 head unit and iPhone.

## ANCS authorization and reconnect

- The first and only iOS Central connection uses `RequiresANCS=true`.
- PAIR and the encrypted B3 challenge stay on the original retained owner.
- Helper does not send `ANCS-READY` until `CBPeripheral.ancsAuthorized` is true.
- `didUpdateANCSAuthorizationFor` resumes the same owner after the iPhone permission decision.
- Android subscribes to mandatory Notification Source first, then serializes Data Source 150 ms later.
- Early Notification Source frames are buffered until both CCCDs are active.
- ATT status 3 is reported as missing iPhone ANCS permission and retried on the same `BluetoothGatt`;
  the physical connection, GATT server, namespace and telemetry relay remain intact.
- Green requires iOS authorization, Helper confirmation, both ANCS CCCDs, B4 CCCD and valid
  battery/network telemetry.

## Vehicle controls

- The stock KX11 trunk action remains visible in the launcher catalog as “Багажник” even when
  ECARX reports an early `notavailable`.
- It is displayed as checking/unknown until the live capability probe succeeds.
- The command path remains fail-closed: no trunk write is sent unless the exact function and zone
  are supported by the vehicle.
