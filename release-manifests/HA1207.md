# Status Widget v2.8.2-ha1207 — Android post-READY client attach

Android-only recovery release for the Geely Monjaro/KX11 Android 9 head unit. The application
package, stable F04/F05 UUIDs, selected-phone identity and signing identity remain unchanged. The
matched repository handoff also includes iPhone Helper v42 and its separate
`verify-helper-v42.yml` workflow; the Android HA1207 implementation and `verify-ha1207.yml`
workflow remain Android-scoped and do not modify the Helper sources.

Helper v42 is the matched companion for this release: it sends PAIR and ANCS-READY with checked
ATT write responses and retains the same exact RequiresANCS owner through the encrypted B3 read.
Its independent macOS workflow compiles the unsigned simulator target and runs the v42 contracts.

## Root cause proved by the 2026-08-10 23:41–23:42 Android trace

- At `23:41:08.823`, HA1206 allocated a direct `clientIf` immediately after F04 publication,
  before PAIR, B3 or ANCS-READY. It timed out at `23:41:18.830`; a second pre-proof allocation ran
  at `23:41:20.339` and timed out at `23:41:30.341`.
- The valid current-link transcript began only at `23:42:15.455`: PAIR opened epoch 3, the first
  B3 read returned ATT status 5 at `23:42:15.501`, encrypted B3 succeeded at `23:42:16.578`, and
  ANCS-READY arrived at `23:42:16.688`.
- The PAIR path nevertheless allocated another direct client at `23:42:15.465`, still before B3
  and READY. That wrapper never produced a connection callback, timed out, retried twice, and the
  final attempt ended with server DISCONNECTED plus client status 133 at `23:42:45.470–45.475`.
- The first causal break is therefore the reverse-role `connectGatt` allocation before the exact
  current F04/PAIR/B3/READY ownership transcript completed. Discovery gating alone was too late:
  the premature Android client registration could already poison or contend for `clientIf`.

## Android HA1207 behavior

- F04 publication, bonded CONNECTED callbacks and PAIR only retain an exact candidate. They issue
  no `connectGatt`, close, retry, RSSI or discovery command in the managed reverse route.
- PAIR, B3 and READY admission, proof commit and checked ATT response execute on the same main FIFO
  as CONNECTED/DISCONNECTED. Protocol transactions are queued before diagnostic logging. UI,
  listener, candidate-adoption and other slow work run only after the ATT response.
- Managed B3 proof is accepted only through the status-5 B3 READ challenge and its encrypted retry.
  A plain `ANCS` write cannot manufacture secure proof.
- READY requires a write-with-response. After proof commit and a successful ATT response, one
  immutable session/epoch/publication/server-peer/raw-facade capture is delayed by the existing
  400 ms response-drain barrier. Any disconnect or tuple rollover makes it a no-op.
- That delayed capture exclusively owns attempt #1. Manual reconnect, recovery, RSSI, discovery,
  close and retry paths cannot race the barrier; a separate per-tuple first-attempt capability
  remains consumed even when the normal bounded retry counter is replenished after full ANCS READY.
- Exactly one READY latch may schedule or consume the first attach for a tuple. Duplicate PAIR or
  READY writes neither reset the bounded attempt counter nor enqueue another immediate attach;
  later attempts use only the normal post-READY retry path.
- A failed non-idempotent PAIR response invalidates the complete downstream B3/READY transcript,
  delayed task and callback lineage. A failed READY response rolls back its new proof/latch and
  cannot allocate a client.
- Address equality cannot transfer ownership between framework `BluetoothDevice` facades. Only an
  exact-object duplicate is idempotent; a new same-address facade starts a fresh epoch and must
  repeat status-5 B3 and READY. A coalesced anonymous alias is explicitly retired.
- Client callbacks must match the active wrapper and its exact session/epoch/publication/raw-facade
  attempt lineage before mutating state or consuming retry budget. Older wrappers are quarantined
  or closed as stale; a retained established owner has one explicit no-op handoff branch.

- Preserved HA1197–HA1206 transport, launcher, climate and icon contracts must remain green.
- HA1207 contracts must prove zero pre-READY client commands, PAIR → status-5 B3 → encrypted B3 →
  checked READY ordering, the 400 ms captured attach barrier, complete rollback, duplicate-budget
  stability, exact raw-facade ownership, anonymous-alias retirement and stale callback rejection.
- The complete unsigned `geelyRelease` APK must assemble with version `v2.8.2-ha1207`
  (`208021207`). Signing remains outside the repository with the stable Android update key.
- Physical validation remains required: install in place, leave Bluetooth and both system pairs
  untouched, verify zero Android `connectGatt` lines before accepted READY, then verify client
  CONNECTED, ANCS discovery, both mandatory CCCDs, B4 and one real notification.
