# Status Widget HA1178 + iPhone Helper v20

Package: `ru.natro.statuswidget`

Version name: `v2.8.2-ha1178`

Version code: `208021178`

## Media-widget controls

- The volume widget now has independent settings for thumb visibility and proportional thumb
  size. Hiding the thumb gives the track the complete configured width.
- The music-position widget has an adjustable vertical gap between its elapsed/duration label and
  the progress track.
- The complete resized progress-widget frame is a scrub surface. Dragging previews the position
  locally and sends one seek command on release to the exact active media session; the progress
  track remains thumb-free.

## KX11 trunk control

- `Багажник` is available from the shared vehicle-function catalog used by HOME shortcuts and
  Driver Panel actions.
- The integration uses the stock KX11 ECARX protocol: function `0x02210100`, zone `0x20000000`,
  with `0` for closed and `1` for open.
- Opening is converted to an idempotent target and always requires an explicit confirmation.
  Closing is immediate.
- The launcher icon follows confirmed ECARX readback and switches between closed and open trunk
  artwork.

## Compatibility

- Existing HA1177 media layouts are migrated without resetting their colours, positions or
  edge-to-edge surface settings.
- iPhone Helper v20, ANCS, B4 telemetry, Classic Bluetooth and the system LE bond are unchanged.
