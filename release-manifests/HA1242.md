## Helper 60

- Lock Screen control titles are 14 pt bold instead of 10 pt semibold.
- Dynamic Island control titles are 12 pt bold instead of 9 pt semibold.
- Long function names may use two centered lines; minimum scaling is raised from 0.68 to 0.82.
- Control values, icons and three-stage indicators are enlarged with bounded tile heights.
- Two simultaneous Live Activities coalesce telemetry updates to one transaction per card every
  three seconds instead of every 750 ms; the newest state is retained and demo taps request an
  immediate flush.
- Temperature and the N mark retain explicit width, single-line and scale bounds.
- `XPC connection invalid`, `CUIThemeStore` and gesture-gate diagnostics are emitted by iOS/CoreUI;
  they are not treated as Natro connection failures. ActivityKit payload and update failures remain
  recorded in Natro's exportable journal.

## Preserved behavior

- Android ANCS Route-A recovery, C5 wire format, package identity and stable signing certificate
  remain unchanged from 2.2.7.
- Bundle ID, App Group and Core Bluetooth restoration identifiers remain unchanged.
- Demo controls continue to alter the same state shown by the in-app dashboard and both cards.
- Physical iPhone/KX11 acceptance remains a release gate after source and hosted Xcode checks.
