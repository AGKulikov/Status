# Status Widget HA1128

## Stable selected-iPhone connection

- Retains the HA1127 saved-MAC lifecycle modelled after Hardware GPS:
  `connectGatt(autoConnect=false, TRANSPORT_LE)`, one owned GATT client, deterministic close, and
  unlimited bounded 2/5/10/20/30/60-second reconnect backoff.
- Keeps BLE scanning and head-unit advertising out of normal operation. A second advertised head
  unit name would not create a second independent iPhone connection and would add another radio
  owner; the paired classic profiles and the exact-device ANCS GATT remain separate transports
  under one saved phone identity.

## Notification controls

- Adds all 12 ANCS category switches.
- Adds application filtering with `all`, `only selected`, and `all except selected` modes,
  including stable keys for known and newly observed iPhone applications.
- Adds an independent color for the temporary notification ticker.
- Adds a configurable one-shot low-iPhone-battery warning with its own color. Its latch survives
  Bluetooth and service restarts, rearms only after recovery above the threshold, and preserves
  the existing Now Playing layout.

## Phone telemetry audit

- Adds explicit profile blocks for HFP, MAP, BLE, and ANCS readiness.
- Adds Battery Service 1.1 `Battery Level Status` (`0x2BED`) read/notification support alongside
  legacy Battery Level and Battery Power State.
- Exposes battery level source, charging source, exact-versus-estimated charging, external power,
  charge state, and charge-level condition.
- Uses cached Android Bluetooth charging metadata as a read-only fallback. If the phone exposes
  only battery percentages, estimates charge/discharge direction from two fresh changed samples
  and labels the result as estimated.
- Adds privacy-safe HFP call state, direction, conference state, audio connection, wideband audio,
  voice-assistant activity, and in-band ringtone blocks. Phone numbers and subscriber identifiers
  are deliberately not collected.
- Makes every scalar field selectable both in the PHONE_STATUS row and in Information blocks,
  with readable Russian presentation of protocol values.

## Driver live climate shortcut

- Replaces the airflow pictograms and fan glyph/segment artwork with the exact resources from the
  user-supplied `MonjaroPanel_2.0.5.apk`, while retaining nine manual and five AUTO speed levels.
- Adds a per-shortcut vertical gap between temperature/fan information and the optional
  AUTO/airflow section.
- Stores the live climate presentation independently from the primary action. The same live tile
  can launch any application, built-in action, vehicle command, smart-home scenario, or Android
  Intent; long press remains independently assignable.
- Migrates existing `stock_climate` shortcuts to the independent live-presentation flag without
  resetting their saved appearance or order.

## Upgrade compatibility

- Built directly on HA1127 and preserves package `ru.natro.statuswidget`.
- Uses version code `208021128` and the stable HA update certificate, so it installs over
  HA1122–HA1127 without deleting settings.
